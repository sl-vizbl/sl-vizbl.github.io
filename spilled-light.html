About the work

It starts as the drawing every art student has made at some point: a cube, a tin mug and a tall jug, shaded in graphite until the surfaces read as plaster and metal. Then the mug tips over, and what pours out of it is not graphite.

Voss drew the still life by hand over three weeks and added the pour last, in a single session, as luminous violet pigment. Up close the stream is full of tiny points of light. From across the room it is the only colour on the wall.

“I wanted one thing in the room that refuses to behave.”

Mira Voss, in conversation with STILLE, 2026

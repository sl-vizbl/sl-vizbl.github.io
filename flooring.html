About this floor

Heritage Oak is milled from slow-grown European oak and pressed into a three-layer engineered board: 4 mm solid oak wear layer over a birch core. The basketweave format lays as individual fingers, so patterns can run classic, herringbone-adjacent, or completely custom.

The golden brown tone comes from a light thermal treatment rather than stain — colour runs through the wear layer and survives two full sandings.

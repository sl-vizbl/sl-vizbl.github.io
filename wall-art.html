About this print

Part of Hokusai's series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, the Great Wave has hung in more homes than any artwork in history — and it still stops people mid-sentence. Our edition is printed from the Metropolitan Museum's open-access scan, colour-matched against the original woodblock impression.

Every print is checked by hand, signed with an edition stamp on the reverse, and shipped with a certificate of provenance for the scan source.

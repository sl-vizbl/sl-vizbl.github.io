About this gown

Six layers of silk-blend tulle over a satin underskirt give the Aurélie its volume without a hoop, so it stays soft to the touch and packs down for travel. The bodice is gathered by hand and finished with crystal and pearl beading across the bust and waistband — every gown takes about eleven hours of beadwork.

Boned corset panels at the back hold the fit without pulling, and the concealed zip runs under a modesty placket. Ordered a size up? The waistband has 4 cm of let-out built in, and hem alterations are included.

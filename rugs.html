About this rug

Oushak rugs were woven in western Anatolia for Ottoman courts and, later, European drawing rooms. This piece carries the classic signature: a spacious terracotta field, ivory border with scrolling palmettes, and that famously silky, lanolin-rich wool that gets softer the longer you walk on it.

It has been professionally hand-washed, checked knot by knot, and comes with minor, honest wear consistent with its age — no repairs, no repainting.

How the demo works

  1. Open a storefront and view its source — every page has two clearly marked comment blocks.
  2. STEP 1 sits in the <head> — paste the Vizbl <script> tag there.
  3. STEP 2 sits right under the “Add to cart” button — paste the trigger element there. To make it look native, add class="btn-cart" to the pasted button (for Core AR's <vizbl-core>: unstyled trigger-class="btn-cart").
  4. Reload the page — the button appears in the store's own design, live and clickable.

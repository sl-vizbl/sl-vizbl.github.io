About this chair

The Fjord is cut from a single sheet of birch plywood, steam-bent so the back leg and armrest flow in one continuous line. The seat, back and armrest pads are upholstered in a tight wool-blend weave that takes daily dinners, desk duty and the occasional coffee splash in stride.

Each chair is pressed and finished in our Porto workshop and ships fully assembled. The oak-veneer surface is sealed with a matte water-based lacquer that keeps the grain visible.
